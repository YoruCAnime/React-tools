# React-tools

## Konosuba Drive Reframer

For the Google Drive folder "konosuba s2-movie-s3-s30va1-2": the anime is enlarged on the left with its subtitles
whole, and the streamers are on the right.

1. Install [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/).
   On Chrome, Edge or Brave, also go to Extensions → Tampermonkey → Details and turn on **Allow user scripts**.
2. **[Install the script](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YoruCAnime/React-tools/main/konosuba-drive/konosuba-drive-reframer.user.js)**
   → click **Install**.
3. Play any video in the Drive folder.

**R** (click the video first) or the small button at the top left switches between Wes and Steph framing,
Freshest framing and the plain video.
